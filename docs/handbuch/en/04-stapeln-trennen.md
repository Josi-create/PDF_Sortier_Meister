# Stacking, splitting and pages

## Stacking PDFs

As in PaperPort: if you drag one PDF onto another, PDFSM puts it **in front**, like a sheet on a
pile. The dragged PDF becomes page 1, the target PDF keeps its name and location. `Ctrl+Z` undoes
the stacking.

## Splitting a PDF

- Take out **all pages individually** or a **page range** into a file of its own.
- **AI-assisted splitting:** a scanned pile with several documents is split at the document
  boundaries. Each document gets a name suggestion and a preview in which the boundaries can be
  adjusted.

## Rotating and straightening pages

- Pages can be **rotated** via the right-click menu of a tile.
- **Right-click › Rotate › "Straighten"** straightens pages that were fed in at an angle.
- For new scans both can happen automatically (can be switched off): pages that are upside down
  or sideways are saved the right way round, skewed pages are straightened. The scanned image is
  not recalculated; a backup copy is made first.

## Text layer

Scanned PDFs without text can get the recognised text as an **invisible layer** (can be switched
off). Then Explorer, Acrobat and other programs find the text too. The scanned image stays
unchanged, a backup copy is made first, and every change can be undone.
